CRISP-DM Data Science Framework

Comprehensive end-to-end documentation across all 6 phases of the Cross-Industry Standard Process for Data Mining.

Phase 1
Business Understanding
Formulate high-precision regression models to estimate trip duration and fare charges for urban passengers, fleet dispatchers, and ride-hailing algorithms. Key business KPIs include minimizing customer fare disputes and achieving RMSLE below 0.25.
KPI: RMSLE < 0.25 ETA Reliability Dynamic Surcharge
Phase 2
Data Understanding
Acquired 74,800+ real records from the Kaggle NYC Taxi challenge repository. Explored coordinate distributions, temporal density across 24 hours, fare skews, and identified critical geospatial boundary constraints.
74.8k Records Geospatial Bounding Temporal Analysis
Phase 3
Data Preparation
Eliminated invalid coordinates and zero-distance outliers. Engineered 23 high-impact features including Haversine and Manhattan distances, compass bearing, distances to JFK, LGA, Times Square, and Wall Street, alongside rush-hour indicators.
Haversine & Manhattan Airport Hub Distances Rush-Hour Flags
Phase 4
Modeling
Constructed dual LightGBM Gradient Boosted Decision Tree models with log1p target transformations to optimize directly for RMSLE and enforce strictly positive predictions, trained alongside a standardized Ridge Regression baseline.
LightGBM Trees log1p Transform Ridge Baseline
Phase 5
Evaluation
Rigorous validation on held-out test splits. LightGBM achieved a 51.5% reduction in Fare RMSE and 52.9% reduction in Duration RMSE over baseline, with 93.2% of fare predictions within $5 of actuals and 99.1% of trip ETAs within 5 minutes.
51.5% RMSE Gain R² = 0.858 (Fare) R² = 0.910 (Duration)
Phase 6
Deployment
Shipped production REST microservices powered by FastAPI and Uvicorn. Fronted by an interactive Leaflet.js dashboard with turn-by-turn road routing, draggable coordinate pins, preset journeys, and live model comparison.
FastAPI Backend Leaflet.js UI OSRM Routing
